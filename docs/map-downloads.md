# RNS315 V12 EU West maps

Look for **RNS315 West Europe V12 2020**, also called **EUR_W_V12**. West includes Norway. Volkswagen lists part number **3AA 051 866 BE** in its [product description](https://vw-sklep.pl/produkty/mapy-nawigacyjne-krajow-europy-zachodniej-rns-315-wersja-v12).

## Map package

[GbcNet: Volkswagen RNS315 West Europe V12 2020](https://www.gbcnet.net/archive/index.php/t-94940.html) is an Italian discussion page linking to a download thread and external file hosts for **Europa Ovest / West**. It is not a Russian site. Attachments or hosts may require an account, and links can change. The file hosts are separate from this repository.

The [download thread is here](https://www.gbcnet.net/showthread.php?94939-Volkswagen-RNS-315-West-Europe-V12-2020-DOWNLOAD). Choose West, extract the archive and look for `navpsf_update`.

## Prepared SD card

If you prefer a prepared card, [Satnavishop in the UK lists RNS315 V12 UK & Europe](https://www.satnavishop.co.uk/vw-rns315-sat-nav-sd-card-update-navigation-map-2020-586-p.asp). Confirm the West region and compatibility before ordering. Follow its supplier's instructions; a prepared card normally does not need preparation with this calculator.

Map data is not included. Obtain maps you are entitled to use. A link to a package does not establish the rights to third-party uploads or authenticate every downloaded archive.

Source pages reviewed on 4 November 2026. Do not use RNS310, RNS510 or Discover Media maps just because a filename also contains Europe or V12.
